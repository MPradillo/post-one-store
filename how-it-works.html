MECHANICAL INTELLIGENCE

No electrical power. No app. No motor. The wet chamber uses reversible manual tumble for washing and a separate higher-ratio SPIN mode for water extraction. The shower supplies water through POST SNAP; gravity handles drainage to the shower floor.

MANUAL POWER

Mechanical drive rather than electronics.

SWITCH & GO

SMALL LOAD

One outfit keeps water, effort and footprint compact.
